About

I'm a mechanical engineering student and Schreyer Honors Scholar at Penn State. I like problems that sit between physical systems and computation, where a differential equation becomes a working machine.

My honors thesis in the Pangborn Advanced Controls Lab (PACLAB) reproduces a commercial 7-inch carbon-fiber quadcopter frame in 3D-printed carbon-fiber filament, with autonomous waypoint flight in a motion-capture space as the goal. Before that I spent a summer at Pittsburgh Drone Services mapping rooftop temperatures for solar siting, and did research at Penn State Behrend on predator-prey population dynamics.

Outside the lab, I'm on the Advanced Vehicle Team's powertrain control module team for the EcoCAR Innovation Challenge, and I manage sponsorship for Infusion, Penn State's intercollegiate South Asian dance competition. Earlier I founded the Penn State Behrend Pickleball Club and spent four years on a MATE underwater ROV team, where I learned that good engineering is as much about coordination and communication as it is about calculation.
