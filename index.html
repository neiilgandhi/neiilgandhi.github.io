I'm a junior mechanical engineering student at Penn State with a focus on dynamics, control systems, and applied mathematics. I'm drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of physical systems and computation — where a differential equation becomes a working machine.

My research at Penn State Behrend modeled predator-prey population dynamics using non-linear ODEs, and I'm currently building a ball-and-plate stabilization platform that uses reinforcement learning to outperform classical PID control.

Outside the lab, I founded Penn State Behrend's Pickleball Club and served as lead mechanical contributor on the MATE ROV team, where I learned that good engineering is as much about coordination and communication as it is about calculation.

I'm a member of Nittany Motorsports (Formula SAE) and Nittany Rocket Labs, and a recipient of the President Walker Award and Behrend Honors Program.
